What the first block is  fake.ReadClientHelloMulti alone, one hello per iteration through an in-memory conn.
  Rows: plain/N=<secrets>/<first|last|miss>/hello=<517|4096>. ns/candidate = ns/op divided by candidates tried
  (1 for first, N for last and miss). The 517-byte hello is the realistic ClientHello size.
What the second block is  BenchmarkDoorHandshake: one whole connection per iteration through ServeConn (dial, entry,
  Telegram dial, end), ee-unpaced and dd, target secret named aaa (first) or zzz (last) among N-1 decoys.

Reading it (baseline, before any change)
  - The unmatched/last scan costs about 4.1 us per candidate at 517 B (about 12 SHA-256 blocks, ~340 ns per block),
    4.1 ms per hello at N=1,000 and about 21 ms at N=5,000, with 6 allocations per candidate. This reproduces the
    3.9 us per secret measured standalone on this box on 2026-10-07.
  - At door level an ee connection costs about 1.0 ms with the target first at any N, about 5.7 ms at N=1,000 and
    about 22.8 ms at N=5,000 with the target last. dd is O(N) too (about 0.49 ms, 3.1 ms, 9 ms); it is not in
    this piece (it has its own limiter).
  - A 4 KiB hello costs about 16-17 us per candidate (N=5,000 miss: 80-88 ms per hello).
